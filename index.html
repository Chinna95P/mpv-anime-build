Anime Mode: Stylized vs. Faithful

This build offers two distinct ways to watch anime. Switch instantly using CTRL + ' (OFF) , CTRL + l (AUTO) and CTRL + ; (ON).

1. Anime4K Mode (ON)

The "Modern" Look

  • Philosophy: "Make it look like 4K."
  • Effect: Aggressively sharpens lines, thins heavy borders, and removes noise.
  • Result: Razor-sharp, highly stylized image. Great for older anime or making 720p look like modern 4K.

2. Reference Mode (OFF)

The "Faithful" Look

  • Philosophy: "Show exactly what the artist drew."
  • Effect: Uses professional scalers (NNEDI3 & FSRCNNX) to smooth lines naturally without altering the art style.
  • Result: Smooth, cinema-quality image preserving line weight, film grain, and background textures.

💡 The "Purist" Workflow

Want Studio-Mastered Quality with Professional Scaling?

1. Press CTRL + ' to disable enhancements (Mode OFF).
2. Press Q to toggle scaling: NNEDI3 (Soft/Natural) or FSRCNNX (Sharp/Clean).

Want Absolute Raw Output?

Press CTRL + g to engage the Master Kill Switch.
This instantly disables ALL shaders (Upscalers, Deband, Sharpening) for a 1:1 bit-perfect render.
(Perfect for A/B testing. Your choice is saved automatically.)
