Custom Buttons Commands

Anime Mode:
1) Power Mode : mp.command("script-message toggle-power-mode")
2) Audio Mode : mp.command("script-message toggle-audio-mode")
Smart-Skip
1) Skip Button: mp.command("script-message smart-skip-audio")
Shaders
1) Shaders Toggle: mp.command("script-message toggle-shaders-mode")
Audio-Output
1) Cycle ao: mp.command("script-message cycle-audio")
2) Reset ao: mp.command("script-message reset-audio")
Ambient-Mode
1) Ambient ON/OFF: mp.command("script-message toggle-ambient")
